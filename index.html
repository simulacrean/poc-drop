    THE CALENDAR
    
        
    
        
    
        
    
        
    
        
    
        
    
        
    
        
    
        
    
        
    Yesterday, today and the days ahead are towns on the road. The world starts as a village and takes years to grow. Drag to look around, click a city to walk there. Skipped days pass on their own; a day you show up for needs a win.
    
      
    

      
      
    
        
    THE COLLECTION 
    
        
    What the box has learned about itself, in the order it learned it. Each artifact waits beside the road on the calendar, a few days after the one before, and only appears once you have found the last. Showing up is the only way to find them. Everyone finds their own.
    
        
    
      
    

      
      
    
        
    THE COSTUME SHOP 
    
        
    Hats, glasses and a moustache from the Guide buddy's sticker drawer. Pay with spare day passes: every day you won, and every day you skipped, counts. Wear one thing on your head, one on your eyes and one on your face. Your robot wears them in fights and on the calendar, and other players see them.
    
        
    
      
    

      
      
    
        
    LOBBY 
    
        
    Everyone who has this page open right now. Insert a POC to get a ticket, then challenge someone who holds one too. You both watch the same fight live, each from your own side.

        MOVE LIST
        
        
          
          
        • DRAG > BOXInsert one POC. It is fingerprinted in your browser, sent to /dev/null, and earns an entry ticket: a shot at one more working day of paid work.
          • 
          
        • FIGHTYour POC's fingerprint fights the newest challenger's. Power, speed and guard decide the rounds, luck breaks ties. Best of three. Win and the ticket becomes a credit: the day is yours.
          • 
          
        • COSTUMESEvery day you win, and every day you skip, earns a pass. Spare passes buy hats, glasses and more in THE COSTUME SHOP: a flower or a bow is cheap, sunglasses and headphones cost more, a top hat or cycling shades is expensive, and the crown is really expensive.
          • 
          
        • ARTIFACTSThe box's discoveries about itself wait beside the road as scrolls, pictures and little boxes, a few days apart and in order. Walk to one to pick it up. Everyone collects their own. They are in THE COLLECTION.
          • 
          
        • RUNNING DRYAt zero the town you are in overheats into a ruin, the box drinks your robot until someone sends coolant (or a day passes), and every day at zero the AIs sell one of your day passes for water.
          • 
          
        • COOLANTThe AIs need water. Walk your robot through lakes beside the road to drink them. Drained lakes refill slowly with rain. The meter drains every day and every fight burns some. Under 25% they overheat; at zero, no fighting until you drink.
          • 
          
        • CALENDAREvery day is a city on one winding road, a little more advanced and a little more dystopian each day. Skip a day and it passes on its own. Show up and you need a win. Walk back to any day you lost and win it back.
          • 
          
        • LOBBYFrom the fight panel when you hold a ticket: see who has the page open right now and challenge them. Both of you need a ticket. You both watch the same fight.
          • 
          
        • WIENER CARWin Operation Cold Aisle in the warehouse, and your robot drives the road in a hot dog car instead of walking. It is faster. Click open land to go off-road: it burns a lot of coolant, and if you run dry you are back on the road where you left it, on foot, with the car parked there. Park it anywhere and walk; come back for it later.
          • 
          
        • K.O.Lose and your ticket is void. You get fired.
          • 
          
        • GRAB + FLINGDrag the mascot and let go to throw him. He does not enjoy it. Drop him in the box and he will climb out, furious.
          • 
          
        • DAY PASSYour ticket pops out of the box. Drag it, flick it, tap it to flip it, or throw it at him.
          • 
          
        • TAP HIMPunch. Head, gut, arms and legs each get a different reaction. Do not aim low.
          • 
          
        • FRI > MONA Friday win buys you Monday. Nobody has checked whether that is allowed.
